Relax & Sleep Better with Golf-Inspired Sounds for Everyone

Dream Links is a premium relaxation and sleep app inspired by the peaceful atmosphere of the golf course—but designed for everyone.

Whether you’re looking to unwind after a long day, improve your focus, meditate, or enjoy deeper, more restful sleep, Dream Links offers a growing library of high-quality audio experiences to suit every mood and lifestyle.

While our signature golf-inspired soundscapes capture the calming beauty of nature, the app also features a wide variety of relaxing sounds including rain, ocean waves, forests, birds, streams, white noise, meditation audio, ambient music, and many more. There’s something for everyone, whether you’re a golfer or simply someone who enjoys peaceful sounds.

Create your perfect listening experience with personalized playlists, smart sleep timers, and beautifully curated sound collections that help reduce stress, improve focus, and support better sleep anytime, anywhere.

At Dream Links, golf is our inspiration—but relaxation is for everyone.
