What is Dream Links?

Dream Links is a relaxation, sleep, and wellness app inspired by the peaceful atmosphere of golf courses. In addition to golf-inspired soundscapes, the app offers a wide variety of calming sounds, including nature, rain, ocean waves, white noise, meditation audio, and more to help you relax, focus, and sleep better.

Is Dream Links only for golfers?

Not at all! While Dream Links is inspired by the tranquility of golf courses, it's designed for everyone. Whether you're looking to reduce stress, improve focus, meditate, or enjoy a better night's sleep, you'll find a wide range of sounds to suit your needs.

How can I download the Dream Links app?

You can download Dream Links from both the Google Play Store and the Apple App Store. Simply click the download buttons on our website or search for Dream Links in your preferred app store.

What types of sounds are available in the app?

Dream Links offers an extensive collection of relaxing audio, including golf course ambience, rain, ocean waves, forests, birdsong, flowing streams, white noise, meditation music, sleep sounds, and many more. New sounds are added regularly to keep your experience fresh and enjoyable.

Is Dream Links available for Android and iPhone?

Yes! Dream Links is available for both Android and iOS devices. Download the app from the Google Play Store or Apple App Store and start enjoying a personalized relaxation experience wherever you are.
