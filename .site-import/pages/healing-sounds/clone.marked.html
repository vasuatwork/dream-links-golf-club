1. What are healing sounds?

Healing sounds are calming audio experiences that can create a peaceful environment for relaxation, meditation, mindfulness, and rest. They may include nature sounds, ambient tones, and soothing soundscapes.

Yes. Calming sounds can help create a quieter and more comfortable environment, making them useful during meditation, mindfulness, relaxation, or after a stressful day.

Yes. Many people include gentle background sounds in their bedtime routine. Dream Links offers relaxing audio that can help create a peaceful atmosphere while you prepare for sleep.
